======================
Pictures: Masterpieces
======================

Famous public-domain paintings, one for each mystery, cropped to squares around the main figures. Click a painting to see all of it. :doc:`Masterpieces </rosary/rosary_artworks>` lists each painting with where to find it.

To use these pictures, choose **Masterpieces** from **Pictures** (under **Options**). To open a page on them, add ``:icons: photo`` to its rosary directive. The pictures are in ``docs\_static\rosary-images``. Click any picture to open it full size.
