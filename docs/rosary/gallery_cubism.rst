================
Pictures: Cubism
================

Each mystery painted in a modernist Cubist style: bold, broken planes of colour held together by strong dark outlines, with the colours of each set chosen to suit its mood.

To use these pictures, choose **Cubism** from **Pictures** (under **Options**). To open a page on them, add ``:icons: photo-cubism`` to its rosary directive. The pictures are in ``docs\_static\rosary-images-cubism``. Click any picture to open it full size.
