========================
The ten steps
========================

Each mystery of the Rosary has ten steps, one for each Hail Mary. Each step
is a short line that tells part of the story, taken from the scripture passage
beside it, so that the ten Hail Marys of a decade walk through the mystery from
beginning to end.

In the rosary tool, choose **The ten steps** under the beads (📖) to show them
during prayer, and tick **Step** under **Read aloud** (in **Options**) to hear
each one before its Hail Mary. Click a scripture reference below to read the
passage (NRSVCE, on BibleGateway).

The Joyful Mysteries
====================

*Prayed on Monday and Saturday.*

1. The Annunciation
-------------------

**First Joyful Mystery.** Fruit of the mystery: *Humility*

1. The Lord himself will give a sign: a young woman will bear a son, Emmanuel.  (`Isaiah 7:14 <https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Isaiah+7%3A14&version=NRSVCE>`__)
2. The angel Gabriel is sent to a virgin named Mary in Nazareth.  (`Luke 1:26-27 <https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Luke+1%3A26-27&version=NRSVCE>`__)
3. Gabriel greets Mary as full of grace: the Lord is with her.  (`Luke 1:28 <https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Luke+1%3A28&version=NRSVCE>`__)
4. Mary is troubled, but the angel tells her not to be afraid.  (`Luke 1:29-30 <https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Luke+1%3A29-30&version=NRSVCE>`__)
5. She will conceive and bear a son and name him Jesus.  (`Luke 1:31 <https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Luke+1%3A31&version=NRSVCE>`__)
6. He will be called Son of the Most High and his kingdom will never end.  (`Luke 1:32-33 <https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Luke+1%3A32-33&version=NRSVCE>`__)
7. Mary asks how; the Holy Spirit will come upon her.  (`Luke 1:34-35 <https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Luke+1%3A34-35&version=NRSVCE>`__)
8. Elizabeth has conceived; nothing is impossible with God.  (`Luke 1:36-37 <https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Luke+1%3A36-37&version=NRSVCE>`__)
9. Mary answers: here am I, the servant of the Lord.  (`Luke 1:38 <https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Luke+1%3A38&version=NRSVCE>`__)
10. The Word became flesh and lived among us.  (`John 1:14 <https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=John+1%3A14&version=NRSVCE>`__)

-------------------------------------
