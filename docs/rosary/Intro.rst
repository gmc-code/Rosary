The four sets of mysteries
==========================

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 30 50

   * - Set
     - Prayed on
     - Mysteries
   * - Joyful
     - Monday and Saturday
     - Annunciation, Visitation, Nativity, Presentation, Finding in the Temple
   * - Luminous
     - Thursday
     - Baptism, Wedding at Cana, Proclamation of the Kingdom, Transfiguration,
       Institution of the Eucharist
   * - Sorrowful
     - Tuesday and Friday
     - Agony in the Garden, Scourging, Crowning with Thorns, Carrying of the
       Cross, Crucifixion
   * - Glorious
     - Wednesday and Sunday
     - Resurrection, Ascension, Descent of the Holy Spirit, Assumption,
       Coronation of Mary

Each mystery has its own picture and ten scripture passages, one for each
Hail Mary of the decade, from the Catholic Public Domain Version. The opening
prayers (Sign of the Cross, Apostles' Creed, Our Father, three Hail Marys and
Glory Be) and the closing prayers (Hail, Holy Queen, Pray for us, the Rosary
Prayer and the Sign of the Cross, with the Prayer to St Michael and the
Memorare if you choose them) are included, so a whole set can be prayed from
start to finish. The prayers are in the wording of the Roman Missal. Any bead
can be read aloud, or the whole Rosary prayed aloud bead by bead, with the
prayers in a recorded female or male voice.
