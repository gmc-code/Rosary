==============================
The mysteries and their fruits
==============================

Each decade of the Rosary begins by announcing its mystery and the *fruit of
the mystery*: the grace or virtue we ask for while we pray it. These are the
words used, so a leader can read them out at the start of each decade when
praying the Rosary with a group or a class.

The rosary tool says the same words on the Our Father bead when
**Mystery and fruit** is ticked under **Read aloud** (in **Options**).


The Joyful Mysteries
====================

*Prayed on Monday and Saturday.*

| **First Joyful Mystery.** The Annunciation. The fruit of the mystery: Humility.
| **Second Joyful Mystery.** The Visitation. The fruit of the mystery: Love of neighbour.
| **Third Joyful Mystery.** The Nativity. The fruit of the mystery: Poverty of spirit.
| **Fourth Joyful Mystery.** The Presentation in the Temple. The fruit of the mystery: Obedience.
| **Fifth Joyful Mystery.** The Finding of Jesus in the Temple. The fruit of the mystery: Joy in finding Jesus.


The Luminous Mysteries
======================

*Prayed on Thursday.*

| **First Luminous Mystery.** The Baptism of Jesus in the Jordan. The fruit of the mystery: Openness to the Holy Spirit.
| **Second Luminous Mystery.** The Wedding at Cana. The fruit of the mystery: To Jesus through Mary.
| **Third Luminous Mystery.** The Proclamation of the Kingdom. The fruit of the mystery: Repentance and trust in God.
| **Fourth Luminous Mystery.** The Transfiguration. The fruit of the mystery: Desire for holiness.
| **Fifth Luminous Mystery.** The Institution of the Eucharist. The fruit of the mystery: Adoration.


The Sorrowful Mysteries
=======================

*Prayed on Tuesday and Friday.*

| **First Sorrowful Mystery.** The Agony in the Garden. The fruit of the mystery: Sorrow for sin.
| **Second Sorrowful Mystery.** The Scourging at the Pillar. The fruit of the mystery: Purity.
| **Third Sorrowful Mystery.** The Crowning with Thorns. The fruit of the mystery: Courage.
| **Fourth Sorrowful Mystery.** The Carrying of the Cross. The fruit of the mystery: Patience.
| **Fifth Sorrowful Mystery.** The Crucifixion and Death of Jesus. The fruit of the mystery: Perseverance.


The Glorious Mysteries
======================

*Prayed on Sunday and Wednesday.*

| **First Glorious Mystery.** The Resurrection. The fruit of the mystery: Faith.
| **Second Glorious Mystery.** The Ascension. The fruit of the mystery: Hope.
| **Third Glorious Mystery.** The Descent of the Holy Spirit. The fruit of the mystery: Love of God.
| **Fourth Glorious Mystery.** The Assumption of Mary. The fruit of the mystery: Grace of a happy death.
| **Fifth Glorious Mystery.** The Coronation of Mary. The fruit of the mystery: Trust in Mary's intercession.
